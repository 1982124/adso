# ADSO AFRICA — Master Architecture

## Brand architecture

- **LÉGENDE VISION**: human vision of positively impacting citizens across Africa.
- **Neo Digital Startup Academy (NDSA)**: company that designs, develops and operates the platform.
- **ADSO AFRICA**: continental technology platform.

## Public positioning

> **ADSO AFRICA — La plateforme africaine pour une mobilité plus sûre et plus responsable.**

> **Éduquer, former et responsabiliser chaque usager de la route.**

Core scope: education routière, formation à la mobilité, prévention, simulation, évaluation et reconnaissance des compétences acquises.

## Strategic institutional positioning

ADSO AFRICA is a complementary African technology and pedagogy infrastructure for citizens, educational organizations, professional sectors, enterprises, communities and institutions. It is designed to support education, prevention, capacity building, learning intelligence and measurable interventions related to mobility and road safety.

ADSO AFRICA does **not** replace ministries, regulators, national road-safety agencies, official examination authorities, national statistical systems or the African Road Safety Observatory. It must never imply governmental ownership, regulatory authority or official endorsement without documented evidence.

## Mobility-first model

ADSO AFRICA is not a digital driving school. Mobility profiles are multidimensional and can coexist:

- Élève
- **Apprenti de tout secteur d'activité**
- Étudiant
- Piéton
- Passager
- Cycliste
- Motocycliste
- Usager de taxi-moto
- Conducteur
- Conducteur professionnel
- Enseignant / formateur
- Établissement / entreprise / institution

### Apprentice principle — mandatory product rule

**Apprenti does not mean apprenti conducteur.** An apprentice in any sector of activity is a road user who may be exposed to traffic risk during commuting, work-related movement, crossings, public transport, two-wheel travel, passenger travel or driving.

The product must therefore treat apprentices as a **priority vulnerable-road-user population**, without assuming that every apprentice drives.

Examples of sectors can include construction, crafts, mechanics, commerce, agriculture, hospitality, services, industry, logistics and other vocational pathways. The exact occupation is contextual; the safety layer is universal.

ADSO must teach apprentices to:

- understand their exposure to road risk;
- identify dangerous situations on the way to and from work;
- protect themselves when walking, cycling or using two-wheelers;
- use public and shared transport safely;
- be visible and predictable around traffic;
- understand worksite/road interfaces and loading/unloading risks where relevant;
- protect colleagues, passengers and other vulnerable users;
- recognize fatigue, distraction, speed and other risk factors;
- make safer decisions without requiring them to become drivers.

## Vulnerable road users

ADSO AFRICA must explicitly include and prioritize people who are exposed to traffic risk without necessarily controlling a vehicle, including children, apprentices, pedestrians, passengers, cyclists, motorcyclists, taxi-moto passengers and other contextually vulnerable groups.

The product taxonomy must separate:

**PERSON PROFILE** → who the user is

**MOBILITY PROFILE** → how the user moves

**RISK EXPOSURE** → where and how the user may be vulnerable

**SKILLS** → what the user can demonstrate

This prevents the product from collapsing every learning journey into driver training.

## Institutional Intelligence

The Institutional layer must support:

- population and cohort management;
- training deployment;
- competency tracking;
- aggregated learning indicators;
- risk-exposure and vulnerable-user learning profiles;
- campaign management;
- before/after measurement;
- reporting and export;
- evidence-oriented program monitoring.

ADSO may measure learning and usage indicators. It must not present learning indicators as official accident statistics or claim accident reduction without validated evidence.

## Country Packs

The architecture targets all 54 African countries. A country remains visible even when its regulatory content is incomplete. Content status can be:

- Disponible
- Enrichi
- À compléter
- À vérifier

National rules should carry source, verification date and confidence/status metadata. Never invent national regulation.

Mali is the first operational proof-of-concept terrain. Benin is a natural second reference terrain. The founder's presence in Mali is a pilot context, not a geographic limitation: the product remains continental and must never be architecturally hard-coded to Mali.

## Africa-native requirements

ADSO AFRICA should be built for African operating realities:

- mobile-first;
- low-bandwidth;
- offline-capable where technically appropriate;
- multilingual;
- locally contextualized scenes;
- configurable payment providers;
- country-specific content;
- privacy-by-design;
- scalable multi-country architecture.

## KPI boundary

ADSO may measure participation, module completion, progression, competencies, assessment results, risk-recognition performance and other pedagogical indicators. It must not present these as official accident statistics or claim causality/accident reduction without validated methodology and evidence.
