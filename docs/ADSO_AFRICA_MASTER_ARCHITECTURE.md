# ADSO AFRICA — Master Architecture

## Brand architecture

- **LÉGENDE VISION**: human vision of positively impacting citizens across Africa.
- **Neo Digital Startup Academy (NDSA)**: company that designs, develops and operates the platform.
- **ADSO AFRICA**: continental technology platform.

## Public positioning

> **ADSO AFRICA — La plateforme africaine pour une mobilité plus sûre et plus responsable.**

> **Éduquer, former et responsabiliser chaque usager de la route.**

Core scope: education routière, formation à la mobilité, prévention, simulation, évaluation et reconnaissance des compétences acquises.

## Mobility-first model

ADSO AFRICA is not a digital driving school. Mobility profiles are multidimensional and can coexist:

- Élève
- Apprenti de tout domaine d’activité
- Étudiant
- Piéton
- Passager
- Cycliste
- Motocycliste
- Usager de taxi-moto
- Conducteur
- Conducteur professionnel
- Enseignant / formateur
- Établissement / entreprise / institution

**Apprenti does not mean apprenti conducteur.** An apprentice is a mobility actor and may be vulnerable as a pedestrian, passenger, cyclist, motorcyclist, taxi-moto user or driver.

## Learning engine

Immersive loop:

`VOIR → DÉCIDER → CONSÉQUENCE → COMPRENDRE → EXERCER → PROGRESSER`

## Institutional boundary

ADSO AFRICA is a complementary technology and pedagogy infrastructure. It does not replace ministries, regulators, national road-safety agencies, official exam authorities or government statistical systems.

Claims about official certification, accident reduction, national regulation or public authority must be supported by appropriate evidence and jurisdiction.

## Country Packs

The architecture targets all 54 African countries. A country remains visible even when its regulatory content is incomplete. Content status can be:

- Disponible
- Enrichi
- À compléter
- À vérifier

National rules should carry source, verification date and confidence/status metadata. Never invent national regulation.

Mali is the first operational proof-of-concept terrain. Benin is a natural second reference terrain. The product remains continental and must never be architecturally hard-coded to Mali.

## KPI boundary

ADSO may measure learning and usage indicators such as participation, module completion, progression, competencies and assessment results. It must not present pedagogical indicators as official accident statistics or claim accident reduction without validated evidence.
