# ADSO — Product Positioning Contract

## Decision

ADSO is a technology platform dedicated to **éducation routière, formation, prévention, simulation et évaluation et reconnaissance des compétences acquises**.

The product has two primary pillars:

1. **Éducation routière et formation**
2. **E-books et contenus éducatifs numériques**

## Explicitly out of the core product

The following domains are no longer primary ADSO products or navigation destinations:

- assurance
- marketplace généraliste
- gestion de flotte
- télématique
- pilotage réglementaire de la sécurité routière

They must not appear in the public home experience, primary navigation, principal CTAs, pricing cards, onboarding, or core marketing positioning.

Existing code and database structures for these domains are **not automatically deleted**. They must first be dependency-mapped, deactivated from the product experience, and migrated/archived safely where appropriate.

## Product truth

Every feature must be classified as:

- LIVE
- PARTIAL
- DEMO
- PLACEHOLDER
- NOT IMPLEMENTED

A feature is never considered complete merely because a route, UI, or database model exists.

## Learning model

PARCOURS → MODULE → LEÇON → SCÈNE → QUESTION → DÉCISION → CONSÉQUENCE → EXPLICATION → EXERCICE → SCORE → COMPÉTENCE → PROGRESSION → RECONNAISSANCE → FÉLICITATIONS

## Recognition model

ADSO evaluates and records learning progress and acquired competencies. It may provide **badges, levels, achievement records, congratulations and attestations of completion** where implemented and legally appropriate.

ADSO must not present itself as a government authority, driving-licence issuer, regulator, or state certification body. Any certificate/attestation generated by ADSO must clearly identify its nature and must never be represented as a government driving licence or state-issued qualification unless ADSO is legally authorized and the authorization is explicitly verified.

Technical legacy structures named `certification` may remain internally where dependency-safe migration is required, but public product language should prefer **évaluation, reconnaissance des compétences, réussite, badge, niveau atteint, attestation** according to the actual feature delivered.

## E-book model

The e-book pillar supports discovery, product pages, preview, purchase, personal library, reading/progress, publishing workflow, and analytics as each capability becomes genuinely operational.

AI-assisted publishing must distinguish generated, checked, approved, published, and archived content. Regulatory claims require traceable sources and verification dates.

## Country data

Country information must carry a verification state and source metadata. No regulatory fact may be invented. Incomplete country data must not silently remove a country from the experience.

## CTO rule

Prefer:

**simplicity > complexity**  
**real product > demo**  
**stability > new features**  
**usefulness > marketing**  
**verified data > invention**  
**recognition of acquired skills > unsupported certification claims**
