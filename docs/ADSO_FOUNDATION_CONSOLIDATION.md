# ADSO AFRICA — Foundation Consolidation

## Canonical architecture rule

`africa-country-directory.ts` is the authoritative African onboarding directory. Legacy `countries.ts` must not be used as the 54-country selector source.

## V1 product truth

- Public education must work without an account.
- Account is required for persistence, personalization, progress, assessment history and protected learner services.
- Learner languages: French, English, Spanish, Arabic, Portuguese.
- Every lesson requires a meaningful visual asset or an explicit media fallback; blank pedagogical cards are not acceptable.
- AI-generated media is valid when provenance is tracked and the asset is presented honestly.
- Regulatory content requires source/version/date governance.
- Françoise is a pedagogical assistant and never an official regulatory authority.
- eBook creation begins with a simple upload and uses AI for enrichment, teaser, metadata and catalog preparation; publication and payment remain governed actions.
- Cockpit mutations require authentication and role authorization.

## Acceptance gate

No V1 freeze until Chains 01–07 are individually production-proven.
