# ADSO — Visual Foundation V1

## CTO decision

The visual backlog is no longer blocked by waiting for documentary photographs. When an exact, legally usable real photograph is not immediately available, ADSO uses an original realistic educational illustration designed for the lesson.

## Mandatory lesson contract

Every priority lesson must have:

**visual → context → optional official sign → interaction → consequence → explanation → audio**

The visual must allow a learner with limited reading ability to identify the actors, situation, danger and expected safe action.

## Audience routing

- **Code de la mobilité:** all apprentices and workers, regardless of occupation.
- **Primary:** children and families.
- **Secondary:** students and young road users.
- **Apprentices:** all occupational apprentices; mobility safety is not restricted to driving.
- **Drivers:** permit and professional driving specialization.
- **Taxi-moto:** professional motorcycle mobility.
- **Fleets:** professional risk prevention.

## Home visual

The official Home visual remains the school-danger scenario specified by the product owner: school, pupil, crossing, school/children sign, parent, motorcycle rider without helmet and clearly understandable danger. It must remain visually dominant and must not be obscured by UI controls.

## Integrity rule

This document is a production specification, not evidence that every listed visual is already integrated. An item becomes **DONE** only after asset storage, code reference, build, deployment and visual verification.

## AI asset policy

Original AI illustrations are acceptable for ADSO when they improve pedagogical clarity. They must be stored and identified as original AI assets and must never be presented as documentary photographs of a real event.

## Country rule

Official regulatory signs and rules remain country-aware. AI may illustrate a generic learning situation, but it must not invent a country's official sign design. Country-specific sign assets require verification against an authoritative source.
