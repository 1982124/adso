# ADSO AFRICA — Autonomous Institutional Engine

## CTO objective

ADSO AFRICA must be capable of generating qualified inbound institutional interest across Africa without requiring the founder to be physically present in the country.

This is an **inbound/value system**, not an automated cold-outreach or influence system.

## Core loop

`VERIFIED SOURCES → COUNTRY INTELLIGENCE → RELEVANT CONTENT → DISCOVERY → INSTITUTIONAL VISIT → DEMAND SIGNAL → INBOUND REQUEST → AI QUALIFICATION → BRIEFING → HUMAN RELATIONSHIP → PILOT → EVIDENCE → PUBLIC LEARNING → NEW DISCOVERY`

## Non-negotiable diplomatic rules

1. Never impersonate an institution, government, ministry, agency or official.
2. Never claim partnership, endorsement, funding or authorization without a recorded formal agreement.
3. Never scrape or target private/personal contact data for unsolicited institutional outreach.
4. Never infer an institution's needs as facts; distinguish verified facts from hypotheses.
5. Never manufacture urgency, public pressure, testimonials, statistics or demand.
6. Never use a public program's beneficiaries as a commercial acquisition list without authorization.
7. Never publish sensitive institutional intelligence that is not appropriate for public use.
8. Every factual institutional claim should retain provenance: source, publication date, country, verification status.
9. AI-generated interpretation must be clearly distinguishable from verified source facts in internal records.
10. A human must approve consequential institutional communications and formal partnership proposals.

## Country discovery layer

Each Country Pack can expose a public, useful country intelligence surface:

- mobility context;
- vulnerable-user education topics;
- apprenticeship/education context when verified;
- official resources;
- relevant public initiatives;
- ADSO educational resources;
- demonstration entry points;
- institutional contact request.

A country page must remain useful even when evidence is incomplete. Missing information is marked `À vérifier` or `À compléter`; it is never invented.

## Content automation

The editorial engine may:

- detect newly synchronized official information;
- classify relevance to ADSO topics;
- draft educational/editorial content;
- attach provenance;
- generate country-specific variants;
- schedule publication;
- flag content requiring human review.

The engine must not turn every institutional event into a sales message.

Recommended content mix:

- educational explainers;
- verified news/context;
- practical mobility guidance;
- country intelligence;
- research summaries;
- scenario demonstrations;
- methodology notes;
- case studies only after authorization.

## Institutional demand surface

Every institutional-facing experience should make three low-pressure paths available:

- `Découvrir ADSO`
- `Demander une présentation`
- `Proposer un échange`

Optional fourth path:

- `Proposer un projet pilote`

The user is never told that their institution needs ADSO.

## Inbound pipeline

### Stage 0 — anonymous discovery

Signals: country page visit, content reading, immersive demo, resource download.

### Stage 1 — declared institutional interest

The visitor explicitly identifies an organization or institutional role.

### Stage 2 — qualification

AI summarizes only what the requester declared and what verified public sources support.

Outputs:

- relevance: low / medium / high;
- topic;
- declared context;
- evidence;
- suggested neutral response;
- next action;
- diplomacy cautions.

### Stage 3 — human review

A responsible ADSO operator reviews the request before a consequential proposal or commitment.

### Stage 4 — briefing

Generate a concise briefing:

- country;
- institution;
- declared role;
- declared objective;
- relevant ADSO capabilities;
- verified public context;
- open questions;
- items that must not be assumed;
- recommended diplomatic posture.

### Stage 5 — relationship

Meeting, demonstration, technical exchange or discovery workshop.

### Stage 6 — optional pilot

Only after mutual interest. A pilot is scoped around a measurable problem and agreed responsibilities.

### Stage 7 — evidence

Learning and implementation evidence can become a case study only with appropriate authorization and privacy safeguards.

## AI agent responsibilities

### Country Intelligence Agent
Maintains verified country context.

### Editorial Agent
Creates useful content from verified material.

### Institutional Qualification Agent
Processes inbound requests.

### Diplomatic Briefing Agent
Prepares internal meeting briefs.

### Opportunity Signal Agent
Detects inbound intent signals and prioritizes them without inventing opportunities.

### Evidence Agent
Tracks provenance and prevents unsupported claims.

### Human Gate
Approves consequential external institutional communications.

## Institutional pull metrics

The system should measure:

- countries generating institutional visits;
- institutions requesting information;
- qualified inbound requests;
- request-to-meeting conversion;
- meeting-to-pilot conversion;
- time to first human response;
- content-to-inbound contribution;
- repeat institutional visits;
- country diversity of inbound demand.

Do not optimize only for lead volume. Prefer **qualified, relevant and consented institutional interest**.

## Remote-first requirement

A country must be able to discover and engage with ADSO without physical representation.

Minimum remote flow:

`COUNTRY PAGE → DEMO → INSTITUTIONAL REQUEST → AI QUALIFICATION → BRIEFING → VIDEO MEETING → PILOT → REPORT`

Physical presence is optional.

## Founder role

The founder should progressively spend less time on prospecting and more time on:

- strategic decisions;
- high-value institutional relationships;
- product vision;
- evidence review;
- major partnerships.

The platform handles discovery, content, qualification and preparation.

## Definition of done

The engine is considered operational only when a real external institution can:

1. discover ADSO from a country-relevant public surface;
2. understand the product without founder intervention;
3. submit an institutional request;
4. receive an immediate neutral acknowledgement;
5. have the request stored and qualified;
6. trigger an internal alert/briefing;
7. receive a human follow-up;
8. progress toward a demonstration without the founder being physically present.

Strategy documents alone do not count as completion.
